AquaBalance ("the app") is developed by Luiz Paulo L. B. Frederico. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary: the app has no accounts, shows no ads and does not send your pool data to us.

Data stored on your device

Pools and spas, water test readings, doses you mark as added, your products and reminders are stored only on your device. We do not have servers and we cannot see this data.

Backups

  • Android Auto Backup: if device backup is turned on, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • Manual export: when you export a CSV or backup file, you choose where to save it (for example Drive, Downloads or email). The file goes only where you send it.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google Play tells the app whether you have an active purchase, and the app stores that status on your device. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage data

The app does not collect analytics or crash reports. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first and you will be able to turn it off in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only to show the reminders you create.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or files access.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Your choices

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings.
