English — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Hushear ("the app") is developed by Luiz Paulo L. B. Frederico. In short: no account, no ads, and your diary, frequencies and sounds stay on your phone. Hushear is a wellness and relaxation app; it is not a medical device.

What the app uses and why

DataWhat happensWhy
Tinnitus diary (loudness, bother, sleep, factors you tick, notes)Stored only on your phoneSo you can follow how you feel and see patterns
Frequencies you matched, presets, sound sessions (start, end, how they ended)Stored only on your phoneFor tailored sounds, your presets and the report
Settings (theme, text size, timer, volume limit)Stored only on your phoneSo the app works the way you set it
SoundsGenerated on your phone. The app has no microphone access and records nothingTo play the sounds
PDF report and data exportCreated on your phone. They leave it only when you tap Share or choose where to save the file, and go only to the app or place you pickTo share with your doctor or keep a copy
Relaxation exercises read aloudYour phone's text-to-speech reads the text. The app only uses voices that work offlineTo guide the exercise
Purchases (Premium)Processed by Google Play. The app only checks whether you own PremiumTo unlock Premium

The app itself has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK, shows no ads, does not use your advertising ID and does not collect your name, email, contacts or location. The only exception is Google Play, described below.

Third parties

  • Google Play handles purchases under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The Google Play Billing Library included in the app sends Google your purchase history and technical diagnostics about purchases.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is on, Android may back up the app's diary, presets, frequencies and settings to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Premium status is not backed up (Google Play restores it).

Ratings and feedback

  • The app may ask you once to rate it on Google Play, using Google's in-app review (Google decides whether to show it). The app never learns whether or how you rated it.
  • In Settings › Feedback and contact you can email us. Your email app opens with a message you can edit; it includes only the app version, Android version, device model and language, to help us fix problems — never your diary, frequencies or sounds. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use your email only to reply.

Permissions

Playing in the background (a media notification keeps the sound on with the screen off), internet (only for Google Play purchases) and wake lock and network state (used by Android components for the media notification and the home-screen widget). The app does not ask for microphone, location, contacts, camera or storage access.

Your choices

  • You can export your data at any time (Settings › Your data) and delete it all by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. There is no account or server, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.

Health

Hushear does not replace a medical evaluation. If your tinnitus is sudden, in one ear only, pulsating, or comes with hearing loss or dizziness, see a doctor.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app changes how it handles data. Questions: lpfr3d@gmail.com.
