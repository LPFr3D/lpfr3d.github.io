English — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

UV Index & Tan ("the app") is developed by Luiz Paulo L. B. Frederico. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: no account, your data stays on your phone, and the free version shows one ad banner (removed with Premium).

What the app uses and why

DataWhat happensWhy
Approximate location (only if you allow it) or a city you typeCoordinates are rounded to about 1 km (2 decimal places) on your phone and only that rounded value is sent to the MET Norway weather serviceTo get the UV forecast for where you are
Skin type, sunscreen preferences, settingsStored only on your phoneTo calculate your safe sun time
Sun sessions, history and tan diary (notes and photos)Stored only on your phone. Diary photos are copied inside the app and excluded from Android backupsSo you can see your history and progress
Purchases (Premium)Processed by Google Play. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own PremiumTo unlock Premium

The app itself does not collect your name, email, contacts or precise location, and it has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The only exception is the ad banner described below.

Third parties

  • MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute, api.met.no) receives the rounded coordinates and your device's network address when the forecast is requested, as with any internet request. Forecast data is licensed under CC BY 4.0. See MET Norway's terms and privacy information at met.no.
  • Google Play handles purchases and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The Google Play Billing Library included in the app sends Google technical diagnostics about purchases.
  • Android backup: if you have Android backup turned on, Android may back up the app's settings and history to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Diary photos are never included.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows one banner ad at the bottom of the home screen, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and share your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your network address, device information and ad interactions, under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). In regions that require it (such as the EEA and the UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Premium removes the banner, and the ad SDK is not started for Premium users. The app's own data (skin type, sessions, diary) is never shared with advertisers.

Notifications and alarms

If you allow notifications, the app shows the morning UV, session alerts and reminders. They are created on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback" or "Report a problem", your email app opens with a message that includes the app version, Android version, device model and language. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use your message only to reply and improve the app.

Your choices

  • You can deny location and type a city instead.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time.
  • You can export your data (Settings › Your data) or delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Since there is no account or server, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Health notice

The app gives approximate estimates and does not replace medical advice. Sun exposure increases the risk of skin cancer.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app changes how it handles data. Questions: lpfr3d@gmail.com.

Terceiros

  • MET Norway (Instituto Meteorológico da Noruega, api.met.no) recebe as coordenadas arredondadas e o endereço de rede do aparelho quando a previsão é pedida, como em qualquer acesso à internet. A versão grátis mostra um banner de anúncio na parte de baixo da tela inicial, fornecido pelo Google AdMob. Para mostrar e medir os anúncios, o AdMob pode coletar e compartilhar o ID de publicidade do aparelho, a localização aproximada obtida pelo endereço de rede, informações do aparelho e interações com os anúncios, conforme a Política de Privacidade do Google (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Nas regiões que exigem (como o Espaço Econômico Europeu e o Reino Unido), o seu consentimento é pedido antes, e você pode mudá-lo a qualquer momento em Ajustes › Privacidade dos anúncios. Você também pode redefinir ou apagar o ID de publicidade nas configurações do Android. O Premium remove o banner, e o SDK de anúncios nem é iniciado para quem é Premium. Os dados do próprio app (tipo de pele, sessões, diário) nunca são compartilhados com anunciantes.

Notificações e alarmes

Se você permitir notificações, o app mostra o UV da manhã, alertas da sessão e lembretes. Eles são criados no seu celular; nada é enviado a um servidor.

Feedback

Se você tocar em "Enviar feedback" ou "Relatar um problema", o seu app de e-mail abre com uma mensagem que inclui a versão do app, a versão do Android, o modelo do aparelho e o idioma. Nada é enviado a menos que você mesmo envie esse e-mail. Usamos a sua mensagem só para responder e melhorar o app.

Suas escolhas
