English — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Offtag ("the app") is developed by Luiz Paulo L. B. Frederico. In short: no account, your blocking data stays on your phone, and the free version shows one ad banner (removed with Premium).

What the app uses and why

DataWhat happensWhy
Name of the app on screen (accessibility service)Read on your phone to decide whether to close it. In strict mode, the service also checks whether a Settings or store screen shows the name "Offtag". It never reads what you type. Nothing from the screen is stored or sentTo block the apps you chose
Modes, chosen apps, schedules, alarms, settingsStored only on your phoneSo the app works
Blocking sessions and attempts to open blocked apps (app name and time)Stored only on your phoneFor your statistics
NFC tags and QR CodesOnly a scrambled fingerprint (salted hash) is stored. The card's ID and data are never stored. The camera image used to read a QR Code is analyzed in memory and discardedTo recognize your tag
Recovery codeOnly a salted hash is storedTo unlock if you lose your tag
Purchases (Premium)Processed by Google Play. The app only checks whether you own PremiumTo unlock Premium

The app itself has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not collect your name, email, contacts or location. The exceptions are Google Play and the ad banner described below.

Third parties

  • Google Play handles purchases under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The Google Play Billing Library included in the app sends Google technical diagnostics about purchases.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is on, Android may back up the app's settings, modes, sessions and statistics to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Premium status is not backed up (Google Play restores it).

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows one banner at the bottom of the Home screen, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and share your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your network address, device information and ad interactions, under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Where required (such as the EEA and the UK), you are asked for consent first and can change it at any time in Settings › Ad privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Premium removes the banner, and the ad SDK is not started for Premium users. Your blocking data is never shared with advertisers.

Permissions

NFC (read your tag), camera (only to read a QR Code, when you choose it), notifications (protection warnings and the alarm), alarms and full-screen notifications (the NFC alarm), starting after a reboot (to resume schedules and alarms) and the accessibility service (blocking).

Your choices

  • You can turn off the accessibility service, notifications or camera access at any time in Android settings.
  • You can delete all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. There is no account or server, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app changes how it handles data. Questions: lpfr3d@gmail.com.
